Privacy policy

FlipCards Hybrid AI - Last updated: November 26, 2026

FlipCards is a flashcard app for studying project management vocabulary. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. This page explains what stays on your device and what can leave it.

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue in the FlipCards Hybrid AI repository. Please do not include personal data in a public issue.

Stored on your device

Your study language, chosen card source, progress, custom cards and sets, theme, AI routing choice and the on-device/cloud call counter are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device. Clearing the site's data removes them.

AI that runs on your device

When Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano model is available, recall checks, hints, memory hooks and custom-card definitions run locally by default. In that case the card text and anything you type for that task are processed by Chrome on your device and are not sent anywhere by FlipCards.

AI that runs in the cloud

Exam drills and comparisons use the cloud by default, and any task falls back to the cloud when on-device AI is not available, unless you set routing to "On-device only" in your profile. A cloud request sends the card term, its short definition, the text you typed for that task (for example, your explanation of the term) and your study language to the Gemini API through Firebase AI Logic. Google processes that request under the Firebase and Gemini API terms. FlipCards does not store it. Every answer in the app shows whether it ran on the device or in the cloud.

Abuse protection and configuration

  • Firebase App Check uses reCAPTCHA Enterprise to confirm that cloud AI requests come from this app. reCAPTCHA may collect browser and device signals under Google's terms.
  • Firebase Remote Config downloads settings such as which model to use and where each task runs. Firebase creates an installation ID for this.
  • Vercel hosts the static files and may keep standard request logs.

Children

FlipCards is a general study tool and is not directed at children. It does not create accounts or profiles.

Changes

This policy changes when the app's features, providers or data practices change. The date at the top shows the current version.
